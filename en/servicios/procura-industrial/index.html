Industrial Procurement

INNOVEN provides procurement of materials and equipment for industrial projects and operations, coordinating the technical and commercial enquiry based on the available requirement information.

Scope and applications

Share technical information about the facility or requirement to guide the enquiry.

  • Material or equipment requisitions for industrial facilities.
  • Requests for electrical components, instrumentation and control.
  • Requirements for insulation, refractories, sealing and related supplies.

Operating coverage

Zulia is the main operating area. INNOVEN handles requirements and works throughout the country.

Available solutions

  • Materials and equipment for industrial projects
  • Electrical components and instrumentation
  • Materials for thermal insulation and refractories
  • Gaskets, joints and industrial sealing solutions
  • Spare parts and supplies related to technical requirements

Indicative enquiry process

  1. Receipt of the requisition, specifications, quantities and available documentation.
  2. Indicative review of application, operating conditions and required technical data.
  3. Coordination with Purchasing and Procurement to coordinate the commercial enquiry.

Information to request a quote

This information helps route the enquiry through Purchasing & Procurement:

  • Technical description, application and required quantity.
  • Available data sheets, specifications, material lists or drawings.
  • Required brand, model or equivalent, when applicable.
  • Required date, delivery location and contact details.

Frequently asked questions

What information improves a procurement request?

A clear technical description, quantities, data sheets, specifications, brand or model when applicable, and required date.

Does procurement cover only one type of material?

No. It can cover requirements related to electrical systems, instrumentation, insulation, refractories, sealing and other industrial supplies.
