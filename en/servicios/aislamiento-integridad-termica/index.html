Thermal Insulation & Integrity Engineering

INNOVEN brings together solutions for heat service, cryogenic cold, acoustic control and refractories, focused on energy conservation, asset protection and operational continuity.

Scope and applications

Share technical information about the facility or requirement to guide the enquiry.

  • Piping, equipment and services that need to retain heat.
  • Cold systems and cryogenic service.
  • Control rooms and equipment where acoustic control is required.
  • Furnaces, boilers, steel structures and vessels that require refractory or Fire Proofing solutions.

Operating coverage

Zulia is the main operating area. INNOVEN handles requirements and works throughout the country.

Available solutions

  • Industrial thermal insulation
  • Insulation for cold and cryogenic service
  • Acoustic insulation
  • Refractory systems
  • Structural Fire Proofing

Materials and technical scope

  • Fiberglass, calcium silicate, expanded perlite and mineral wool.
  • Ceramic fiber for insulation and refractory applications.
  • Foam Glass, polyurethane, neoprene and expanded polystyrene for cold and cryogenic service.

Indicative enquiry process

  1. Collection of available operating conditions and documentation.
  2. Review of the technical scope, material or system to be assessed.
  3. Coordination of the enquiry with the relevant area and identification of missing information.
