Industrial Refrigeration & HVAC

INNOVEN provides design, installation and maintenance of comfort, precision and chilled-water air conditioning systems for industrial facilities.

Scope and applications

Share technical information about the facility or requirement to guide the enquiry.

  • Civil and industrial buildings that require comfort air conditioning.
  • Areas with precision or chilled-water systems.
  • Facilities that require related design, installation, maintenance or supply.

Operating coverage

Zulia is the main operating area. INNOVEN handles requirements and works throughout the country.

Available solutions

  • Comfort, precision and chilled-water air conditioning
  • Galvanized sheet and polyurethane ductwork
  • System design and installation
  • Maintenance and supply of original equipment and spare parts
  • Specialized staff available 24/7/365

Materials and technical scope

  • Galvanized sheet ductwork.
  • Polyurethane ductwork.
  • Original equipment and spare parts, according to the requirement.

Indicative enquiry process
