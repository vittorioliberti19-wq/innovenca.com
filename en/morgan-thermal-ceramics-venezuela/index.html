Morgan Thermal Ceramics in Venezuela

INNOVEN is the official representative of Morgan Advanced Materials – Thermal Ceramics for thermal insulation solutions and refractory materials in Venezuela.

Scope and applications

Share technical information about the facility or requirement to guide the enquiry.

  • Thermal insulation and passive fire protection in industrial environments.
  • Lining and insulation of furnaces, boilers and process equipment.
  • Applications that require shaped refractories, monolithics or microporous insulation.

Operating coverage

Zulia is the main operating area. INNOVEN handles requirements and works throughout the country.

Available solutions

  • Fibers, blankets, modules, papers, felts, boards and shapes
  • Refractory bricks and shapes
  • Insulating, dense and special-use monolithics
  • Microporous insulation
  • FireMaster passive fire protection systems

Indicative enquiry process

  1. Identification of the application, equipment, operating conditions and available documentation.
  2. Indicative review of the material, shape or system required for the enquiry.
  3. Coordination with Purchasing and Procurement; availability and final selection depend on the requirement assessment.
